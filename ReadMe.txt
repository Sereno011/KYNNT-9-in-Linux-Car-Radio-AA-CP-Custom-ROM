This is a patched ROM for a 9" Linux based AA/CP capable car radio.

Mainboard PN: PCY-8800-02H-V3.1
BIOS chip is a cFeon QX128A-104HIP (EN25QH128 compatible).


This model does not support loading custom wallpapers and booth logos via USB.

**Factory Code** [8215]

Stock backup copy also included. But make your own backup as well.
A programmer will be required. (ie; CH341A + AsProgrammer utility)
*Note: NeoProgrammer failed to write with this model's specific BIOS chip.

--Change log

Replaced all 7 stock wallpapers. They were too bright making text font difficult to read on display. Replacements are a mixture of darker tone gradients.

Swapped 7 of the OEM boot logos. European specific brands replaced with:
Infiniti, Jeep, RAM, GMC, Mercury, Scion, Acura

Removed: Skoda, MG, Citroen, Renault, Geely, BYD, Peugeot
